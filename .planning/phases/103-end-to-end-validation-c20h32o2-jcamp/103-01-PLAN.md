Measured against the REAL files during research (2026-07-26). These are facts, not guesses.

1. **HMBC cannot be read at all today.** `JcampReader.read_2d()` on the real
   `C20H32O2_HMBC.dx` raises
   `ValueError: Implausible 13C ppm axis: [-4.57, 234.81] outside expected [-15.0, 230.0]`.
   The 234.8 ppm figure is a **legitimate, wider HMBC acquisition window**
   (`$OFFSET=234.8062`, SW ≈ 30091 Hz at `SF=125.705` MHz), not a units bug — verified by
   monkeypatch that widening the bound yields a physically sensible axis
   (F1 13C `[-4.57, 234.81]`, F2 1H `[-0.45, 7.05]`). Task 1 is a **prerequisite** for
   everything else.

2. **Default knobs massively over-pick on the real full-size matrix.** At the shipped
   `snr_floor=5.0`: HSQC 10,687 cross-peaks (expected ~17–27), COSY 16,244, HMBC 19,015
   (after the Task-1 fix), 1D 13C 45 peaks (§10 lists 20), 1D 1H 265. Root cause:
   `_compute_2d_noise_sigma`'s global MAD measures max/sigma ≈ **53,615:1** on this real
   CS/IRLS matrix versus the ≈3,477:1 of the Phase-99 mock it was calibrated against —
   ~15× higher dynamic range. Full verdict at defaults: **FAIL** on `quaternary_exclusion`
   (326 HSQC correlations land at the 5 quaternary shifts — a symptom of over-picking, not
   a real quaternary leak) plus soft `edited_sign_consistency`.

   Measured HSQC count vs. `snr_floor` (`detect_negative=True`): 5→10687, 10→3448,
   20→828, 50→340, 100→219, 200→121, 300→98, 500→61, 800→57, 1000–2000→~50–52 (plateau),

   0.02→51, 0.05→46.

3. **`threshold` and `snr_floor` are MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE MODES, not combinable.**
   `PeakPicker2D.pick_peaks` and `bridge_peak_pick` both do `use_snr = threshold is None`
   — passing an explicit `threshold` for an experiment silently disables SNR mode and
   `snr_floor` is ignored **for that experiment**. Same in `bridge_peak_pick_1d`. The
   per-experiment option design must respect this; the help text must state it.

4. **The `detect_negative` flag is derived by the bridge, not by the caller**
   (`multiplicity_edited, _ = detect_multiplicity_edited(spectrum.data)` for HSQC only,
   `False` for HMBC/COSY). **Therefore the knob sweep must call `bridge_peak_pick(...)`,
   NOT `PeakPicker2D.pick_peaks(...)` directly** — only then does the swept count equal
   the count the governed CLI run will produce. (103-RESEARCH.md's Pattern-2 example calls
   the picker directly with a hardcoded `detect_negative=True`; that would be a different
   number. Use the bridge.)

5. **`qc_report.json` is NOT written on a PASS/PARTIAL run.** `cli/jcamp.py` writes it
   only in the FAIL branch, into the quarantine dir. On PASS/PARTIAL the full report exists
   only in the `--format json` stdout under the `report` key. To satisfy D-11.3 the
   executor must capture stdout and write the report out itself.
