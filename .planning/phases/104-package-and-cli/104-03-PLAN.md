## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-104-06 | Tampering | `lucy_deprecated` stdout | mitigate | The hint is hard-coded through `click.echo(..., err=True)`. Test 2 compares real-subprocess stdout bytes between alias and ailsa, and the Task-1 smoke check compares against the pre-rename golden files. |
| T-104-07 | Tampering | webview launcher | mitigate | The 2-tier `_build_launcher` never execs a lone `lucy` (possibly stale old-package code); covered by Test 2 of test_webview_launcher.py. |
| T-104-08 | Denial of service | `case.md` `$(lucy webview serve … 2>&1)` merges stderr | accept | That output is read by an LLM orchestrator as free text (it extracts the URL). One extra warning line does not break it, and `.claude/` is out of scope until the skill-system phase. |
