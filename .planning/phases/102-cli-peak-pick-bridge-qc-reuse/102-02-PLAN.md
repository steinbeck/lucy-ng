<objective>
Ship the one genuinely new piece of picking glue this phase needs: a thin 1D
bridge that turns a `Spectrum1D` (from `JcampReader.read_1d`) into the exact
JSON payload `cli/pick.py::pick_1d` emits, so the unchanged Phase-99 QC gate
can discover it as trusted 1D reference.

Purpose: D-03 makes 1D picking non-optional. Without committed 1D peak lists
in the peaks directory, `nus/qc.py::QcReferenceData.resolve()` reports
`insufficient_reference_data` on a pure-JCAMP dataset and the whole QC-reuse
story collapses. The schema contract is the safety-critical part: a mismatch
is SILENT (`_load_1d_shifts` does `data.get("peaks", [])`, defaulting to an
empty list rather than raising), so it must be proven by a real
`QcReferenceData.resolve()` run, not by inspection.

Output: `src/lucy_ng/processing/jcamp_1d_bridge.py` exporting
`bridge_peak_pick_1d`, plus `tests/test_jcamp_1d_bridge.py`.
</objective>
